Interactive Feeding Cost Calculator

Compare premium feeding costs tailored specifically to your adult dog. Select a diet, enter your dog's target weight, and see exactly what it costs to feed per day and per meal.

Adult Dogs Only:
This calculator and comparison matrix are designed for adult dogs only. We may add dedicated guides for puppies and seniors in the future.

The 'All Life Stages' Advantage:
The foods we proudly stock (Wild Pet Food, The Innocent Hound, and JR Complete Wellbeing) are approved for All Life Stages. This means puppies, adults, and seniors can all safely enjoy them! (The Lily's Kitchen ranges shown below are formulated specifically for adults).

Why Lily's Kitchen?
For feeding amounts and cost comparison purposes, we have chosen Lily's Kitchen dry and wet adult ranges to compare to the foods we offer. We have chosen Lily's Kitchen as it is one of the most popular and well known brands in the UK. Please note, we do not sell Lily's Kitchen, it is here for comparison purposes only.

About these calculations:
All feeding amounts shown are a calculated approximation of the manufacturers' daily feeding guidelines, which can often be confusing and difficult to compare side-by-side. You should always refer to the feeding guide on the manufacturer's label for their stated feeding amounts, as some of the figures here have been calculated to harmonise this cost calculator and matrix. Every dog is different and some will need to eat more and some less to maintain a healthy weight.

  • Activity levels must be factored in. Less active dogs need less food, more active dogs need more food.
  • Treats should always be deducted from the daily feeding amounts.
  • Fresh water should always be available to your dog.
