Wild Pet Food: Superior Cold-Pressed Nutrition

When we made the deliberate decision to elevate our range of complete diets, Wild Pet Food was an immediate and uncompromising choice. It represents the pinnacle of modern canine nutrition, moving entirely away from heavily processed kibble to deliver a diet that works in perfect harmony with your dog's digestive system.

The Cold-Pressed Advantage

The majority of commercial dog foods are extruded, meaning they are blasted with extreme heat and pressure. This process severely depletes natural nutrients and creates a hard biscuit that swells rapidly in a dog's stomach.

Wild Pet Food does things differently. Their ingredients are gently pressed at significantly lower temperatures. This crucial difference achieves two vital things:

  • Maximum Nutrient Retention: The lower temperatures lock in the natural vitamins, minerals, and essential oils that standard manufacturing destroys.
  • Superior Digestion: Cold-pressed food does not swell. It breaks down gently and rapidly in the stomach, making it exceptionally comfortable to digest and significantly reducing the risk of bloat.

Uncompromising Ingredients

Crafted right here in the UK, every Wild recipe is built upon a foundation of premium, responsibly sourced proteins. There are no cheap fillers, no artificial colours, and absolutely no hidden nasties.

Whether your dog requires a strictly grain free recipe or simply a cleaner, more natural diet, Wild offers a naturally hypoallergenic, deeply nourishing solution designed to support healthy skin, bright eyes, and stable energy levels.
