The Innocent Hound: Luxury Air-Dried Nutrition

When curating our premium selection, The Innocent Hound stood out as a true artisanal masterpiece. Handcrafted in Yorkshire, this range represents the ultimate evolution in convenience for discerning dog owners, perfectly bridging the gap between raw feeding and everyday practicality.

The Art of Air-Drying

Traditional cooking methods often strip ingredients of their natural vitality. The Innocent Hound takes a completely different approach. Their recipes are gently air-dried at low temperatures for up to seven days.

This meticulous process draws out the moisture to prevent spoiling, while completely preserving the raw integrity, natural vitamins, and intense flavours of the ingredients. The result is a highly digestible, incredibly hygienic alternative to raw food that can be stored straight in the cupboard.

The 70% Original Recipes

We proudly stock their signature 70% Original Recipes. Built on a foundation of single-source, British-farmed meats, these completes are exceptionally rich in quality protein.

Because the air-drying process concentrates the nutrients, you feed significantly less than standard kibble. It is a dense, highly palatable diet that is exceptionally brilliant for fussy eaters, dogs with sensitive stomachs, or those who simply deserve the absolute best in their bowl.

Premium Sausage Treats

Alongside their complete diets, we carry a hand-picked selection of their legendary air-dried sausages. Made with the same uncompromising standards and high meat content, these are not your average pet shop treats. They are a high-value, luxury reward that dogs absolutely go wild for.
